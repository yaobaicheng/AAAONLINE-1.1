FAQ

Questions About Copper Tubes
in Indonesia

Stock, sizes, matching accessories and lead time — ask us directly. Here are the questions we hear most often.

What products do you supply in Indonesia?

We keep Hailiang copper pancake coils, straight copper tubes, insulated copper tubes, branch pipes, straight connectors, elbows and tees in stock, together with insulation materials, PU duct panels, accessories and refrigerants such as R22, R32, R410A and R134A. Please contact our sales team for current sizes and stock.

What copper tube sizes are commonly available?

Common copper tube sizes used in air-conditioning and refrigeration include 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 5/8", 3/4", 7/8", 1", 1-1/8", 1-1/4", 1-3/8", 1-1/2", 1-5/8" and 1-3/4". They are supplied as pancake coils or straight lengths, and wall thickness can be selected according to working pressure and project requirements. Tell us your project requirements and quantity, and we will help match the right sizes.

How do I choose a branch pipe?

Branch pipes are selected by the number of indoor units, each unit's capacity and the main/branch pipe diameters. For multi-split systems, match them against the manufacturer's capacity and pipe-size tables so the branch line is not undersized. We can help select the right model from your system drawing and indoor-unit list.

Do you keep stock, and how fast can you deliver?

We hold stock of popular copper tube sizes, insulation materials and refrigerants in our Indonesian warehouse. In-stock items can usually be released soon after order confirmation; the exact lead time depends on size, quantity and delivery area, so please confirm with our sales team before ordering.

How do I match insulation with copper tubes?

The insulation's inner diameter should match the copper tube's outside diameter — for example, a 9.52 mm tube needs the matching insulation size. Insulation thickness depends on ambient temperature, humidity and condensation risk. Send us your copper tube list and we will quote a complete set.

Can you deliver across Indonesia, and which languages do you support?

We can arrange delivery to your project address; freight cost and lead time are confirmed when you order. Our team communicates in Chinese, English and Indonesian via WhatsApp or WeChat.

How can I verify the quality of YIMIAO HUANYU products?

All products we supply come through regular purchasing channels, and we check specifications, packaging and batch information on arrival. If you need technical data or quality documents, tell us the model and application before ordering, and we will provide the documents available for the actual batch.
